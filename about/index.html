Justin Bartlett is a Grammy-winning producer and audio engineer. Born and raised in Massachusetts, he came up in Philadelphia under Will Yip at Studio 4, the Conshohocken room whose history runs from the Fugees and Bob Dylan to Billy Joel and Kris Kross.

When Yip opened Memory Music Studios, his new flagship studio in Philadelphia, Bartlett made the move with him. It is where he produces, records, mixes and masters today.

Alongside Yip he has worked with Turnstile, Movements, Turnover, Circa Survive and The Wonder Years, among many others. That run includes Turnstile’s Never Enough, winner of the Grammys for Best Rock Album and Best Metal Performance.

As a saxophonist he has shared stages with Turnstile and Turnover, from the Brooklyn Mirage and the Stone Pony Summer Stage to Citizens Bank Park, the Newport Jazz Festival and Boston’s Symphony Hall.

He produces as a musician first. The hours at the console are spent with the band rather than apart from it: on the sound, the performances, and the small decisions that make a record.
